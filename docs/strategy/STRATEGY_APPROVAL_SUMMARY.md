## 2. Key Strategic Decisions Requiring Explicit Approval

The following major strategic decisions are submitted for stakeholder signoff:

| Decision Ref | Decision Title | Proposed Recommendation | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DEC-006** | **Market Positioning Model** | Adopt **Alternative C: High-Integrity Systems Engineering & Resilient Digital Infrastructure House**. | Protects parent company sovereignty; perfectly aligns with CAC MEMART objects; avoids reducing Asabiyyah to a single product or unbacked AI lab. |
| **DEC-007** | **7-Route Corporate Sitemap** | Adopt a focused 7-route architecture: `/`, `/about`, `/services`, `/products`, `/engineering`, `/contact`, `/legal`. | High information density without sprawl; provides dedicated landing destinations for enterprise clients, partners, and organizers. |
| **DEC-008** | **Dila Regulatory Disclaimer** | Mandate an unmissable disclaimer on all Dila pages: *"Dila is a pure software platform. Dila does not take custody of funds, issue loans, or operate as a bank. Monetary flows route via licensed CBN commercial banks."* | Prevents regulatory misclassification under CBN guidelines and establishes institutional trust. |
| **DEC-009** | **Exclusion of Site Search** | Omit an in-page search bar on the website. | A focused 7-page site with clean navigation does not require client-side search; omitting it saves bundle weight and eliminates maintenance overhead. |
| **DEC-010** | **Monogram Headshot Fallback** | Use styled geometric monogram badges (`MAA`, `SS`, `MRZ`) in visual mockups until official executive studio headshots are provided. | Preserves zero fabrication; enables visual progress without holding back UX/UI design. |
