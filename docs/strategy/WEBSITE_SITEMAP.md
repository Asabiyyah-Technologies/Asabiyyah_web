# Website Sitemap & Information Architecture: Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd

This document defines the complete structural hierarchy, navigation patterns, URL taxonomy, and page-by-page section blueprints for the official Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd corporate website.

---

## 1. Information Architecture Principles

1. **Clarity Over Sprawl:** Avoid diluting visitors' attention across dozens of thin pages. Prefer rich, authoritative, high-density pages with clear cross-linking.
2. **Sovereign Corporate Positioning:** The primary navigation immediately frames Asabiyyah as an engineering technology company, positioning products as evidence of that capability.
3. **Frictionless Conversion:** Every page provides direct, context-aware pathways to start an engineering conversation, request a pilot, or connect with leadership.
4. **Transparent Governance:** Clear placement of corporate credentials (RC 9918400, registered office, terms, privacy).
