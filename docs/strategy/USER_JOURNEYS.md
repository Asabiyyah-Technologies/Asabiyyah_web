## 2. Journey Map 1: Prospective Commercial Enterprise Client (B2B)

- **Persona:** *Tunde / Amina* — Chief Technology Officer or Head of Operations at a Nigerian commercial mid-market firm or logistics enterprise.
- **Mindset / Pain Point:** Struggling with broken, manual field processes or poorly written third-party software that crashes under high volume or bad networks. Needs a serious engineering partner who understands local operational realities.
- **Entry Point:** Homepage (`/`) or Services (`/services`) via referral or industry search.
- **Step-by-Step Flow:**
  1. Lands on `/`: Sees the authoritative headline *"Building Technology That Matters"* and concrete proof of systems engineering.
  2. Scans *Core Capabilities*: Identifies *Custom Software Engineering* and *Business Process Automation*.
  3. Navigates to `/services`: Reads specific problems solved, deliverables, and technical capabilities (FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL).
  4. Clicks to `/engineering`: Validates engineering rigor (offline-first paradigms, data security, NDPR compliance).
  5. Clicks *"Start an Engineering Conversation"*: Enters the structured `/contact` form, selects *"Commercial & Enterprise Software"*, provides project parameters, and submits.
- **Conversion Metric:** High-intent project inquiry submitted with clear business scope.

## 3. Journey Map 2: Cooperative Leader / Thrift Group Organizer (Dila Pilot)

- **Persona:** *Alhaji Ibrahim / Grace* — President of a registered Multi-Purpose Cooperative Society or leader of an active 40-member market Adashe group in Kano/Kaduna.
- **Mindset / Pain Point:** Overwhelmed by paper ledgers, manual reconciliation errors, chasing late members on WhatsApp, and disputes over collection balances.
- **Entry Point:** Direct link from community referral, social video, or Products page (`/products`).
- **Step-by-Step Flow:**
  1. Lands on `/products` (or Home Dila spotlight): Recognizes familiar problem context (*"Africa’s Primary Capital Engine Runs on Paper & WhatsApp"*).
  2. Reviews Dila capabilities: Notes deterministic payout schedules, automated member tracking, and printable QR collection cards.
  3. Reads regulatory clarity: Notes with relief that Dila is a transparent software ledger and does not take custody of funds, routing through licensed commercial banks.
  4. Clicks *"Request Pilot Access"*: Form auto-fills inquiry category as *"Dila Cooperative Pilot Access"*; specifies group size (e.g., 30 members) and operational city.
- **Conversion Metric:** Pilot application submitted for onboarding into closed-cohort testing.

## 4. Journey Map 3: Institutional Partner / DFI / Civic Grant Officer

- **Persona:** *Dr. Sarah* — Program Director at an international development finance institution (DFI), civic tech fund, or election integrity organization.
- **Mindset / Pain Point:** Needs an authentic, highly capable local technology team in Nigeria to deploy civic intelligence tools, voter protection systems, or educational AI frameworks. Wary of superficial marketing startups.
- **Entry Point:** `/products` (AMANA SHIELD or ClassGPT-N) or `/about`.
- **Step-by-Step Flow:**
  1. Lands on `/products`: Inspects the AMANA SHIELD architectural overview and WhatsApp integration mechanics.
  2. Navigates to `/engineering`: Inspects the multi-pillar risk fusion logic, adversarial prompt defenses, and PostgreSQL tsvector search.
  3. Clicks to `/about`: Reviews statutory corporate governance (CAC RC 9918400, registered Jigawa office) and executive leadership credentials (CEO First Class Comp Sci, verified AI certifications).
  4. Clicks *"Inquire About Institutional Partnership"*: Directs to `/contact` with institutional scope.
- **Conversion Metric:** Formal partnership consultation scheduled.

## 5. Journey Map 4: Technology Collaborator & Prospective Engineering Talent

- **Persona:** *Fatima / David* — Senior full-stack or AI engineer looking for a serious, mission-driven engineering organization in Nigeria that writes world-class code.
- **Mindset / Pain Point:** Tired of superficial fintech clones and crypto hype; wants to work on hard systems problems (offline-first sync, local quantized LLMs, double-entry accounting).
- **Entry Point:** `/engineering` or `/about` via technical community link (NACOS / GitHub / LinkedIn).
- **Step-by-Step Flow:**
  1. Lands on `/engineering`: Immediately impressed by the technical depth — reads detailed explanations of SQLite edge sync, HMAC webhook verification, and Alembic migration patterns.
  2. Navigates to `/about`: Reads the philosophy of *'Asabiyyah* and leadership profiles.
  3. Reviews open repositories and CEO GitHub (`MaAbumusAb`).
  4. Navigates to `/contact`: Submits an inquiry under *"General / Talent Inquiry"*, attaching portfolio and GitHub link.
- **Conversion Metric:** Qualified engineer connects with the leadership team.

## 6. Journey Map 5: General Visitor / Academic Due Diligence

- **Persona:** Curious observer, student, or local journalist wanting to understand what Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd does.
- **Step-by-Step Flow:**
  1. Browses `/` and `/about`.
  2. Gains clear, jargon-free understanding of the parent company, its three distinct product initiatives, and its Nigerian roots.
  3. Follows public links or subscribes to company announcements.
- **Conversion Metric:** Accurate brand perception established without confusion.
