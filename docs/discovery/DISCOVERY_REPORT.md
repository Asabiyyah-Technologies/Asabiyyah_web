## 3. Critical Boundary Controls & Anti-Fabrication Safeguards

To maintain absolute institutional credibility and adhere strictly to the project's Working Principles:

1. **Dila Positioning Guardrail:**
   - **Enforced Rule:** Dila must **never** be marketed as a bank, digital neobank, lender, wallet, or deposit-taking institution.
   - **Accurate Presentation:** Position Dila as a **"Community Finance OS & Rotating Savings Platform"** providing code-enforced double-entry ledger software for independent group organizers and cooperatives. Monetary transactions route directly through licensed commercial banks and CBN-regulated PSPs.
2. **Zero Fabrication of Metrics:**
   - We will not cite unverified metrics (e.g., claiming 1,000 active groups or $15k MRR, which are 18-month targets).
   - Accurately state: *"Undergoing closed-cohort alpha pilot validation with active group organizers in Northern Nigerian commercial clusters."*
3. **Parent Brand Sovereignty:**
   - Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd's corporate site will feature the parent brand identity (Navy `#1B2A4A`, Gold `#C8A951`, Crescent Swirl mark, and tagline *"Building Technology That Matters"*).
   - Dila, AMANA SHIELD, and ClassGPT-N will be showcased within a dedicated, structured product portfolio section.
