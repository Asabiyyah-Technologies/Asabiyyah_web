## 4. Statutory Objects of the Company

Under Clause 3 of the Memorandum of Association (MEMART) registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission, Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd is legally empowered to carry out:

1. **Technology Development:** Design, development, deployment, operation, licensing, maintenance, and commercialisation of software, mobile applications, web applications, and digital platforms.
2. **Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Systems:** Machine learning systems, automation tools, data analytics, decision-support systems, natural language technologies, and agentic AI systems.
3. **Sectoral Digital Platforms:** Development and operation of technology solutions for individuals, businesses, institutions, communities, and associations.
4. **Technology Education & Capacity-Building:** Training, capacity building, and digital transformation services.
5. **Applied Research & Innovation:** Experimentation and product development in emerging and applied technologies in collaboration with academic and industry partners.
6. **IT & Enterprise Infrastructure:** Software engineering, systems integration, cloud and digital infrastructure services, and technology consulting.
7. **Commercial Partnerships & Joint Ventures:** Lawful commercial agreements, licensing, and strategic partnerships.

- **Asabiyyah Technologies Ltd** is the corporate technology house, holding the IP, engineering capabilities, legal standing, and enterprise consulting offerings.
