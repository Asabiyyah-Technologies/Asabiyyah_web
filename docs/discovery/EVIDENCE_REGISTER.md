This register catalogs every significant corporate, leadership, technical, and product claim, cross-referencing each claim to its primary source document, exact evidence location, confidence rating, and founder confirmation requirement.

---

## Confidence Classification Taxonomy

- **VERIFIED:** Directly evidenced and cross-checked in official legal certificates, regulatory status extracts, or inspected production codebases.
- **REPORTED:** Stated in internal business plans, pitch decks, or CVs, but lacking external regulatory or third-party validation.
- **INFERRED:** Deductions based on consistent patterns across multiple documents.
- **CONFLICTING:** Discrepancies between different documents requiring explicit leadership clarification.
- **UNKNOWN:** Assertions or assumptions unsupported by supplied materials.
