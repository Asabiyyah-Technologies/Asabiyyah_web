## 2. Key Design Decisions Submitted for Approval

| Decision Ref | Decision Title | Proposed Recommendation | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DEC-011** | **Creative Direction Adoption** | Adopt **Direction 1: The Sovereign Technology House**. | Honors *'Asabiyyah* with civilizational dignity; matches official corporate letterhead; establishes institutional credibility with banks, DFIs, and enterprise buyers. |
| **DEC-012** | **Corporate Logo Specification** | Adopt the **Cohesion Crescent Swirl** (blue gradient) paired with uppercase geometric `ASABIYYAH` wordmark. | Distinct, culturally grounded, verified from primary assets; vector format ready. |
| **DEC-013** | **Typographic Pairing** | Adopt **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings and **Inter** for body text and data tables. | Balances architectural authority in headlines with world-class optical legibility and cross-platform reliability in body copy. |
| **DEC-014** | **Color Palette Anchors** | Anchor palette to **Sovereign Navy (`#0A192F` / `#0D2B4E`)**, **Warm Imperial Gold (`#C8960C` / `#C8A951`)**, and **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**. | High-contrast (WCAG AAA `15:1`+), aligns with letterhead and CAMA certificates, avoids generic tech dark-mode tropes. |
| **DEC-015** | **Zero-Disruption Prototype Integration** | Integrate verified Dila UI flows directly into Product Showcase cards while maintaining strict non-bank disclaimers. | Maximizes value of existing 152+ screens without diluting parent company standing. |
